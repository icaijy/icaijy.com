How this list works

Who is includedCodeforces users rated 1200+ (pupil or above) and AtCoder algorithm users rated 1200+, with Australia listed in their public profiles. Handles are independent across platforms; we do not infer shared identities.

Last 30 daysIncludes public contest and practice submissions. “No known AC” means submissions were observed without an accepted result in the cache; it does not mean someone is currently online. Counts use distinct platform accounts. Earlier accepted results count as neither recent ACs nor attempts without a known AC.

Difficulty and cached dataProblems are sorted by difficulty within each platform, hardest first; unknown difficulties come last. AtCoder uses unofficial estimates from AtCoder Problems. Failed updates retain cached data. Private submissions and accounts may not be covered.
